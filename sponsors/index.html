The Opportunity

A festival built on the two minutes we already proved.

Iceland Eclipse took 3,333 people to the edge of the world, guests from 75+ countries, 287 artists and speakers, for two minutes of darkness. Snæfellsnes forced that cap: Hellissandur is a village of 360 people, and demand was turned away at the door.

Midnight Sun brings three to four times that many people into the middle of Reykjavík for three days where the darkness never comes at all. Produced by IMXP, it trades a remote expedition for a city takeover, and a two-minute scarcity moment for roughly seven hours of golden-hour light, every night, that no other festival on the circuit can program around.

New Inventory, Only The City Can Sell

What Reykjavík adds that the wilderness couldn't.

Iceland Eclipse's remote site had no airport, no hotel stock, and no local audience to sell into. Midnight Sun's downtown footprint unlocks categories that simply didn't exist on the glacier.

Airline Partner

Keflavík runs 28 airlines to 80 destinations in summer; Icelandair or PLAY is the most natural title-adjacent category in this market.

Hospitality Group

5,620 hotel rooms and 4,185 short-term rentals across the capital region, running above 90% summer occupancy.

WHITE NIGHT Naming

Venue naming across the 00:00–03:00 downtown club and late-night circuit, walkable from the main site.

FIRST LIGHT Pools

Sunrise programming inside Reykjavík's geothermal pools, 03:00–06:00, golden light through to 05:10.

City & Destination

Reykjavíkurborg, Business Iceland (Íslandsstofa) and Visit Reykjavík as civic and tourism partners.

Geothermal & Renewable
