Event Concept Summary

Iceland Eclipse sold out, taking people to the edge of Iceland to experience Totality.

Midnight Sun will bring up to 10,000 into the heart of Reykjavík for three days where it never gets dark.

Iceland Eclipse was an expedition. Midnight Sun is a city takeover. That inversion, remote to urban, scarcity to accessibility, two minutes of night to seventy-two hours of light, turns a once-in-a-century astronomical event into a repeatable annual business.

The Creative Spine

A festival with no night.

On 9–11 July the sun rises ~03:25 and sets ~23:35, roughly 20 hours of daylight. At solar midnight the sun sits only 3.5° below the horizon: no astronomical night, no nautical night. The darkest moment of the weekend is civil twilight.

The sun holds the golden-hour band from ~21:55 straight through to ~05:10, seven hours of low, raking light. The most valuable production asset the event owns, and it is free.

Nothing in the standard festival playbook works: no lasers, no darkness reveal, no headliner-under-the-stars. Every convention gets rebuilt, which is exactly why it will look like nothing else on the circuit.

The Venue · Hljómskálagarðurinn

The city is the infrastructure.

Reykjavík built a purpose-designed event lawn here in 2023: ISK 120–130m of sports-grade turf, drainage and integrated lighting, made “to receive crowds on major celebration days.” Pride's 80,000-person parade already terminates here. Everything is walkable: no shuttles, no campsite mud, no parking fields.
