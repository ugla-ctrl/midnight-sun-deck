## Assumptions

- Premium tickets: 15% (900 of 6,000)
- Sold w/ accommodations: yes, 4 accommodation nights
- 20% of guests take travel & hotel packages (1,200 of 6,000)
- 5% take tours (300 guests)
- 50% take side events (3,000 guests, avg $100)
- Avg discounting: 5% · Contingency: 10%
- Liability insurance: $3/head · Cancellation insurance: 3.0%
