## Sourcing rules for future edits

1. **Never invent a number or claim.** If it's not in one of the files above, or the doc doesn't say what the deck implies, go check the live link before adding it.
2. **The doc's `[FACT]` / `[ASSUMPTION]` / `[DECISION]` tags matter.** Facts can be stated directly. Assumptions and open decisions should be phrased as such (e.g. "proposed," "working range," "pending") not as settled facts.
3. **The strategy doc's §4 (regulatory) and §9 (Secret Solstice inheritance) are deliberately excluded from the deck.** That's internal risk-planning material, not because it's wrong, it's just not investor-facing copy. Don't add it to the deck without checking with Mitch first.
4. **No em dashes anywhere in deck copy.** House style rule. Use commas, colons, or middots (·) instead.
5. **Mitch's contact email is `mitch@im-xp.com`**, confirmed via his own Slack messages. The partnership deck itself shows two different, older, inconsistent addresses, don't revert to those.
6. **Satellite sponsorship tier is $20,000**, not $50,000, per the strategy doc's own resolution of that conflict (§10.2).
