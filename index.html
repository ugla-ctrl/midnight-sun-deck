Summary

Iceland Eclipse took 3,333 people to the edge of the world for two minutes of darkness.

Midnight Sun brings three to four times that many into the heart of Reykjavík — for three days where the darkness never comes at all.

Same team. Same creative DNA. An expedition turned into a repeatable annual business.

The Experience

A festival with no night.

~20 hours of daylight. ~7 hours of golden-hour light nightly, 21:55 – 05:10 — the signature production asset, and it's free.

Nothing in the standard festival playbook applies — which is exactly why it will look like nothing else on the circuit.
